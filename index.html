About / Research / Writing / Contact

I am an undergraduate at MIT studying CS with a minor in Brain & Cognitive Science. I am currently researching at MIT CSAIL in the Language and Intelligence Group, where my recent focus is on interpretability and reward modeling to better understand internal representations for improving consistency and reliability. Previously, I interned at Letta on the research team, building memory management capabilities in agentic systems, and this summer I will be joining Databricks as an intern. I am also a Venture Fellow with Felicis, exploring the early-stage startup ecosystem.

I am interested in building agentic AI that can act autonomously and learn from their interactions, reflecting on its own reasoning and improving self-consistency through model introspection and reward modeling. My research aims to explore how these systems can learn to generalize, communicate effectively, and learn from guidance, with the broader goal of making AI more trustworthy and beneficial.
